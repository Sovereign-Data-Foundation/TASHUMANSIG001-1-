## John Adams’ Vision for a Balanced Republic

John Adams believed that stable liberty requires a “government of laws, and not of men.” [1] He feared that unchecked wealth would corrupt the republic. [2] His structural solution was a mixed government designed to neutralize economic factions. [2] [1, 2, 3, 4, 5]

## Key Mechanisms of Accountability

- Dual Sovereignty: Power originates from the people at both state and national levels.
- Double Security: Overlapping jurisdictions prevent a single economic class from capturing the entire government.
- Subordinate Status: Corporations and commercial entities exist strictly as chartered creations of law.
- Supremacy of Law: No private interest possesses sovereign immunity or stands above public interest. [6, 7]
